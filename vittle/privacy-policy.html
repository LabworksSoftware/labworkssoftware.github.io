Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1st May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Vittle - Food Expiry Tracker ("Vittle"), published on Google Play by Labworks Software.

Information We Collect

Vittle does not collect, store, or transmit personal information directly.

All data you enter into the app (such as food items, expiry dates, and categories) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Labworks Software.

Notifications

Vittle uses local notifications to remind you about expiring food and to provide relevant in-app updates.

These notifications are generated entirely on your device based on your stored data. No notification data is sent to or stored by Labworks Software.

Advertising

Vittle may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob.

These advertising providers may collect and process certain data, which may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • Device information (such as model and operating system)
  • General usage and interaction data

This data is used to provide, personalize, and measure advertisements.

Labworks Software does not control the data collected by these third parties. For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Future Features and Permissions

Future versions of Vittle may include additional features that require permissions such as:

  • Camera access – for barcode scanning or similar functionality
  • Storage access – for selecting or saving images (e.g. profile pictures)
  • Account sign-in – such as optional Google Sign-In for cloud sync features

If these features are introduced, they will only be used to provide the intended functionality. No personal data will be collected, used, or shared without your explicit consent.

Data Sharing

Labworks Software does not sell, trade, or share your personal information.

Any data processed by third-party services (such as advertising providers) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data Security

Since Vittle stores data locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

Labworks Software does not have access to your stored data.

Children's Privacy

Vittle is not directed specifically at children, and Labworks Software does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
